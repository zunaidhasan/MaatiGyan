--- DOCUMENT 1 ---
TITLE: Boro Rice Fertilizer Recommendation — Low Organic Carbon Soils
CROP: Boro Rice (বোরো ধান)
SEASON: Rabi (Dry Season, November–March)
SOIL_CONDITION: SOC < 1.5%, Nitrogen Deficient
APPLICABLE_DISTRICTS: Bogura, Rajshahi, Dinajpur, Rangpur, Gaibandha, Sirajganj
SOURCE: BARC Fertilizer Recommendation Guide 2023, Table 14

RECOMMENDATION:
- Urea: 180 kg/bigha (vs. common practice of 260 kg — saves ৳840 per bigha)
- TSP (Triple Super Phosphate): 55 kg/bigha
- MoP (Muriate of Potash): 40 kg/bigha
- Zinc Sulfate: 2 kg/bigha (CRITICAL — Barind tract is severely Zn-deficient)
- Gypsum (Sulfur source): 15 kg/bigha if soil pH < 5.5

APPLICATION SCHEDULE:
- Basal (at transplanting): TSP 100% + MoP 50% + Zinc Sulfate 100%
- 1st Top-dress (15–20 DAT): Urea 40%
- 2nd Top-dress (30–35 DAT, panicle initiation): Urea 60% + MoP 50%

NOTES:
- Do NOT apply all urea at once — split application reduces losses by 35%
- Zinc deficiency shows as bronzing of lower leaves
- If waterlogging observed, delay MoP application

SEASON: Kharif-2 (Wet Season, June–November)
SOIL_CONDITION: SOC < 1.5%
APPLICABLE_DISTRICTS: Bogura, Rajshahi, Joypurhat, Chapainawabganj
SOURCE: BARC Fertilizer Recommendation Guide 2023, Table 8

RECOMMENDATION:
- Urea: 130 kg/bigha
- TSP: 40 kg/bigha
- MoP: 30 kg/bigha
- Zinc Sulfate: 2 kg/bigha (apply if no zn applied in last 2 seasons)

APPLICATION SCHEDULE:
- Basal: TSP 100% + MoP 50% + Zinc Sulfate 100%
- 1st Top-dress (15 DAT): Urea 40%
- 2nd Top-dress (35 DAT): Urea 60% + MoP 50%

NOTES:
- Do NOT apply urea if standing water > 3 inches (high N loss risk)
- Aman season benefits from green manure (Dhaincha) incorporation before planting

RECOMMENDATION:
- Urea: 120 kg/bigha

SOURCE: BARC Fertilizer Recommendation Guide 2023, Table 35

RECOMMENDATION:
- Urea: 165 kg/bigha
- TSP: 80 kg/bigha (potato = highest P demand of all crops)
- MoP: 75 kg/bigha (potato = highest K demand)
- Gypsum: 20 kg/bigha

- Apply cow dung 2–3 t/bigha to improve tuber yield and soil structure
- Ridge planting essential for drainage — waterlogging kills potato in 24 hours

--- DOCUMENT 7 ---
TITLE: Soil Acidification Management — pH Correction Protocol
TOPIC: Soil pH Correction and Lime Application
SOIL_CONDITION: Soil pH < 5.5 (acid soils), common in Sylhet, Chittagong Hill Tracts, Madhupur
APPLICABLE_DISTRICTS: Sylhet, Sunamganj, Moulvibazar, Chittagong, Rangamati, Bandarban, Tangail (Madhupur)
SOURCE: SRDI Soil Health Report 2021, Chapter 6; BARC Acid Soil Management Guide

RECOMMENDATION FOR pH CORRECTION:
- Agricultural Lime (Dolomite): 200–300 kg/bigha if pH 4.5–5.5
- Agricultural Lime: 100–150 kg/bigha if pH 5.5–6.0
- Apply lime 2–3 weeks BEFORE fertilizer application
- Retest soil pH after 1 season

ADDITIONAL MEASURES:
- Organic matter (cow dung, compost): 1–2 t/bigha improves buffering
- Avoid ammonium-based nitrogen sources (acidifying) — use urea or nitrate forms
- Green manure incorporation raises pH by 0.2–0.5 units over 2 seasons

NOTES:
- 42% of Bangladesh arable land now has pH < 5.5 (SRDI 2021)
- Acidification accelerated by overuse of urea and TSP
- Zinc availability drops sharply at pH < 5.5 — correct pH before Zn application

--- DOCUMENT 8 ---
TITLE: Zinc Deficiency Management — Barind Tract and River Floodplains
TOPIC: Micronutrient Management — Zinc
SOIL_CONDITION: Zinc deficient soils (Zn < 0.8 ppm by DTPA extraction)
APPLICABLE_DISTRICTS: Rajshahi, Bogura, Chapainawabganj, Naogaon, Natore, Dinajpur, Rangpur
SOURCE: SRDI Micronutrient Survey 2019; BARC Fertilizer Recommendation Guide 2023

SYMPTOMS:
- Rice: Bronzing and necrosis of lower leaves at 15–25 DAT
- Wheat: Chlorotic streaking on new leaves
- Maize: White bud disease

RECOMMENDATION:
- Zinc Sulfate (ZnSO4·7H2O, 21% Zn): 2 kg/bigha basal application
- Foliar spray (0.5% ZnSO4 solution): at tillering stage if deficiency appears
- Frequency: Apply once every 2 seasons (residual effect)

NOTES:
- Barind Tract (NW Bangladesh) — 78% of soils zinc-deficient (SRDI 2019)
- High-yield varieties demand more zinc than traditional varieties
- Never mix Zn sulfate with phosphate fertilizers — apply separately

--- DOCUMENT 9 ---
TITLE: Waterlogging Management — Tidal Floodplain Soils
TOPIC: Drainage and Waterlogging Risk Mitigation
SOIL_CONDITION: Waterlogging risk HIGH, tidal/coastal soils
APPLICABLE_DISTRICTS: Khulna, Bagerhat, Satkhira, Barguna, Patuakhali, Bhola
SOURCE: SRDI Soil Health Report 2021, Chapter 9

CROP ADAPTATIONS:
- Use submergence-tolerant rice varieties: BRRI dhan51, BRRI dhan52, Swarna-Sub1
- Delay transplanting until floodwater recedes (>20 cm water = crop failure risk)

FERTILIZER ADJUSTMENTS:
- Reduce Urea by 30% (waterlogged soils have denitrification losses)
- Avoid Urea on waterlogged soil — use Prilled Urea only when water < 5cm
- No MoP during heavy rain season (K leaching)
- Priority: Phosphate + Zinc basal application only

SALINITY CONCERNS (Coastal Districts):
- EC > 4 dS/m: Switch to salinity-tolerant varieties (BRRI dhan47, BRRI dhan73)
- Gypsum 50 kg/bigha can help leach sodium from saline soils

--- DOCUMENT 10 ---
TITLE: Organic Carbon Building — Green Manure and Compost Protocols
TOPIC: SOC Improvement Strategy
SOIL_CONDITION: SOC < 1.5% (severely degraded soils)
APPLICABLE_DISTRICTS: Rajshahi, Bogura, Dinajpur, Rangpur (Barind region)
SOURCE: SRDI Soil Fertility Management Manual 2020

STRATEGY:
1. Dhaincha (Sesbania rostrata) green manure: Sow 4–6 weeks before transplanting
   - Incorporates 40–60 kg N/bigha equivalent
   - Raises SOC by 0.1–0.2% per season
2. Rice straw incorporation: Do NOT burn — incorporate with Urea 5 kg/bigha
   - Enhances microbial decomposition
3. Poultry manure: 500 kg/bigha (basal) — especially effective for SOC building
4. Municipal compost (if available): 1,000 kg/bigha

EXPECTED OUTCOMES:
- SOC improvement: +0.15–0.25% per year with consistent organic inputs
- Reduced chemical fertilizer need: 15–25% reduction after 3 years
- Improved water holding capacity — reduces irrigation need

TITLE: Bangladesh Rice Variety Guide — Boro Season (High-Yield Varieties)
TOPIC: Variety Selection for Boro Season
SOURCE: BRRI Variety Guide 2023

RECOMMENDED VARIETIES:
- BRRI dhan29: Classic high-yield Boro variety, 180–220 kg N/ha need, 145 days
- BRRI dhan58: Heat-tolerant, suitable for late Boro, 160 kg N/ha need
- BRRI dhan89: Modern variety, zinc-efficient, suitable for acid soils
- BRRI dhan96: Premium quality, 170 kg N/ha need, 140 days
- BINA dhan8: Suitable for saline coastal areas, Boro season

NOTES:
- BRRI dhan29 remains most popular in NW Bangladesh but requires intensive nutrient management
- Higher-yield varieties need proportionally more zinc and potassium

--- DOCUMENT 12 ---
TITLE: Seasonal Fertilizer Calendar — Bangladesh Crop Calendar Integration
TOPIC: Crop Season and Fertilizer Timing
SOURCE: BARC Agronomy Division, 2023

SEASONS:
- Boro (বোরো): November/December planting → March/April harvest (irrigated rice)
- Aman (আমন): June/July planting → October/November harvest (rainfed rice)
- Aus (আউশ): March/April planting → July/August harvest (early rice)
- Rabi vegetables (রবি শস্য): October–February (potato, wheat, mustard, lentil)
- Kharif vegetables (খরিফ শস্য): April–September (jute, vegetables)

DISTRICT CROP CALENDARS:
- Bogura: Boro Rice (Nov–Apr) → Transplant Aman (Jun–Nov) → Mustard (Oct–Dec)
- Sylhet: Transplant Aman (Jun–Nov) → Boro Rice (Dec–Apr)
- Rajshahi: Boro Rice (Nov–Apr) → Aman (Jul–Nov) → Wheat/Mustard (Nov–Feb)
- Khulna: Single Aman (Jul–Nov) → Rabi vegetables or fallow (Nov–Mar)
- Mymensingh: Boro Rice (Dec–Apr) → Aman (Jul–Nov) → Rabi crops (Nov–Feb)
